# Dataset use and attribution

This repository republishes a Tyllus-generated compilation of public U.S. Federal Register notices. The underlying U.S. government notices retain their original status and source URLs.

The MIT License in this repository applies to the refresh script and the repository landing page. It does not add or replace rights for the dataset contents. Review the [Tyllus Terms of Use](https://www.tyllus.com/en/terms-of-use) and the terms of each linked official source before reuse.

When referencing the compilation, please attribute **Tyllus U.S. Import Evidence Change Radar** and link to:

https://www.tyllus.com/en/resources/us-import-evidence-change-radar

The evidence-domain mappings are discovery aids. They are not legal advice, compliance decisions, risk scores or statements that a proposal is in force.
