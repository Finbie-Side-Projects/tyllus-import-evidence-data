# Design

Show useful content immediately: dataset scope and actual snapshot metrics, followed by readable notices. Use a restrained navy/blue palette, warm neutral background, clear typography and a compact editorial layout. Cards must expose publication date, issuing agency, full title, original abstract preview, topic tags and official links. Expand each card to see full source text, effective/comment dates where supplied, docket IDs and the matched taxonomy terms.

Provide search, agency/topic/type filters, sorting, result counts, pagination, explicit empty states and a reset control. Keep downloads and citation available below the explorer. Avoid implementation instructions and internal terms as headings. Do not hide the records behind links to another website.

Support keyboard focus, native labels/details, announcement of result counts, narrow screens without horizontal overflow, and visible light/dark controls.
