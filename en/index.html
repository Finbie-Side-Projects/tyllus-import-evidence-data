                About these notices

                Where the data comes from

                The snapshot preserves the original notices and adds a searchable evidence-topic classification. Here is what each part means.

                01 / Official sources

                Notices come from the Federal Register API for CBP, FMC, ITA, USITC and USTR. Every record links to the official publication and its PDF when available.

                02 / Topic matching

                Tyllus applies a deterministic keyword taxonomy. Expand a notice to see the exact terms that matched each topic. A topic match is a way to find records; it does not determine whether a notice applies to your shipment.

                03 / Dates and coverage

                Publication dates show when notices appeared in the Federal Register. Effective dates and comment deadlines appear only when the source supplies them. This is a dated snapshot; updates are run on demand.

                04 / Scope and interpretation

                This dataset is not a complete inventory of U.S. import requirements. Notices, final rules and proposed rules have different legal effects. Review the official source, its scope and dates before making an operational decision.
